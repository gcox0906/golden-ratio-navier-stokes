\begin{abstract}
We describe a verified replacement of the two-dimensional auxiliary torus
covering in OpenAI's Navier--Stokes construction. The replacement has
determinant 11 rather than 14 and eigendirections in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$.
The complete dependent proofs of the repository's forced whole-space and
periodic challenge theorems were accepted by Comparator, Lean's kernel,
and nanoda. The theorem statements and problem definitions are unchanged.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Attribution and scope}
The original construction, PDE argument, and upstream Lean formalization
are due to OpenAI; see \href{https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler}{NavierStokesAndEuler}
and \href{https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/32d9f210-8b73-45e0-91bc-82a30aef8a9a/navier-stokes.pdf}{Finite time blowup for Navier--Stokes}, Section 6.
This contribution is an alternative auxiliary construction and the proof
repairs needed to integrate it. It does not introduce a new Navier--Stokes
statement, an unforced regularity result, or a black-hole theorem.
The upstream challenge credits the Formal Conjectures authors for the
adapted problem statement; that credit is retained. Lean, Mathlib,
Comparator, lean4export, and nanoda supply the verification infrastructure.

\section*{The replacement}
The original and replacement matrices are
\[
 J_0=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 J_\varphi=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 b=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}.
\]
The replacement has $b^2+b=1$, eigendirections $v_r=(1,-b)$ and
$v_t=(b,1)$, and eigenvalues
\[
 \Lambda=\frac{7-\sqrt5}{2}=3-b,\qquad
 T=\frac{7+\sqrt5}{2}=4+b,\qquad \Lambda T=11.
\]
The patch updates covering maps and degrees, integer quotient calculations,
common-cover coordinates, Fourier inversion estimates, and the spectral
scale parameters throughout the dependent construction. The quotient
$\mathbb Z^2/J_\varphi^d\mathbb Z^2$ has $11^d$ elements; the original
degree is $14^d$. This reduces that combinatorial count, without establishing
a runtime improvement or a stronger final PDE conclusion.

The original $\rho$ already exceeds $1/2$. The added lemma
\texttt{ChartScales.rho\_gt\_half} therefore does not cross a previously
unavailable threshold. No wider range of the PDE parameter $h$, or optimality
over all admissible matrices, is claimed.

\section*{Verification and evidence}
The library build completed with warnings treated as errors: 820 source
modules, including the root. An audit lists 2469 theorem declarations,
including generated declarations, in the 20 changed or added construction
modules. A separate standalone audit covers 59 named theorems and one finite
instance. The final checked challenge targets are
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
NavierStokes.Comparator.navier\_stokes\_breakdown\_R3\\
NavierStokes.Comparator.navier\_stokes\_breakdown\_periodic.
\end{quote}
Comparator ran with the original challenge configuration and
\texttt{enable\_nanoda=true}, exited zero, and reported:
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
nanoda kernel accepts the solution\\
Lean default kernel accepts the solution\\
Your solution is okay!
\end{quote}
It checked equality of the challenge statements and dependent problem
definitions and the permitted-axiom whitelist: \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Quot.sound}, and \texttt{Classical.choice}. No \texttt{sorryAx}
is permitted in the accepted solution. Intentional reference-challenge
placeholders are distinct from solution proofs. Dependency traversal confirms
that both final proofs reach the changed covering and spectral constants.
The independent checker covers their dependent proofs; it does not separately
replay every unused declaration in the library.

The run used Landrun under an unprivileged account in a Linux container.
An inherited seccomp wrapper replaced the documented systemd wrapper;
filesystem denial and an AF\_UNIX denial test were recorded. This note reports
proof acceptance and those tests, rather than a general adversarial-sandbox
certification. The exact scripts, configuration, binary hashes, and logs
are included.
